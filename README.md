Problem Statement:

Many urban dwellers and hobby gardens struggle to cultivate crops effectively due to cultivate crops effectively due to limited physical space, a lack of accessible and structured plant care knowledge, and the difficulty of tracking multiple growth stages simultaneously. Without an organized tool, growers frequently face issues like improper watering schedules, poor space utilization, and missing the right window for harvesting. Additionally, the lack of a dedicated community leaves beginners feeling isolated and unsupported. Cropwise solves this problem by offering an intuitive platform that allows urban gardeners to visually map their garden plots, follow clear care calendars, track plant health throughout their lifecycles, and engage with a local community to share knowledge and harvests.
